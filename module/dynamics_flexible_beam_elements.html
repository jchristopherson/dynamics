euler_bernoulli_beam_element flexible_beam_element timoshenko_beam_element

Energy-based three-dimensional flexible beam elements.

Use a consistent system of length, mass, and time units. Internal forces are the positive gradient of stored energy and the tangent stiffness is its Hessian with respect to the element DOFs.

type, public, extends(two_node_beam_element) ::  timoshenko_beam_element

Finite-strain spatial beam with linear centerline interpolation and independently interpolated section rotations. Axial, bending and torsional energy use four Gauss points; transverse shear uses the midpoint to relieve thin-beam shear locking.
