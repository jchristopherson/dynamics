Static equilibrium and transient dynamics for flexible finite-element meshes.

Both solvers use the mesh's internal force. Static equilibrium also uses the assembled tangent; transient integration uses a constant reference mass and lets the integrator approximate its own Jacobian. Neither solver manages history-dependent material state.
