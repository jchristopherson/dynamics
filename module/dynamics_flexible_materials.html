flexible_material linear_elastic_isotropic_material

Material energy interfaces for flexible elements. Use a consistent unit system: energy density is energy per reference volume, bending and torsional energies are energy per reference length, moduli are stress, and density is mass per reference volume.

type, public ::  flexible_material

Constitutive energy contract for flexible elements. Implementations provide consistent real, dual and hyper-dual 3D energy evaluations and separate axial plane-stress, bending, and torsion laws for beams.
