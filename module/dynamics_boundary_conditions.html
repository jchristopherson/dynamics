Applies force and displacement boundary conditions to the unconstrained equations of a finite_element_mesh. A condition addresses a local DOF of a mesh node. Force conditions are added to the global force vector. Displacement conditions are eliminated from the assembled system and can be restored after the reduced system is solved.


Uses
